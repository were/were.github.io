Bio

I am a 2nd year Ph.D student under the guidance of Prof. Tony Nowatzki. My research interests mainly lie on designing and analyzing heterogeneous reconfigurable accelerators as well as developing compilers for these accelerators.

Prior to joining UCLA, I worked as research intern at Prof. Zhiru Zhang's group for one year, which was the last year of my undergraduate. I got my B.E. degree from ACM Honored Class, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
